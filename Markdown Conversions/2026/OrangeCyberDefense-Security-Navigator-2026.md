## Key Data of the Year

### Terminology: Events, Potential- and Confirmed Incidents
- **Actors**: Entities causing an incident (VERIS framework - [verisframework.org](https://verisframework.org)).
- **Action**: What the threat actor did.
- **Asset**: The asset that was affected.
- **Events**: ELDoXg/XsDR, Network sensors, SIEM, enriched incident data from Core Fusion platform.
- **Potential Incidents**: Customer input or analysts' discretion conclude that security was compromised.
- **Confirmed Incidents**: One or more Events are correlated or flagged for further investigation by human analyst.

### Confirmed Incidents (October 2024 to August 2025)
- 139,373 Potential Incidents
- 19,053 Confirmed Incidents
- Global True Positive rate = 13.7%

#### Breakdown by Category:
- **Actors**: 56.9% Internal, 39.1% External, 3.5% Other, 0.5% Partner.
- **Action**: 44.6% Misuse, 30.8% Hacking, 12.9% Other, 6.3% Error, 5.2% Malware, 0.2% Social, 0.1% Environment, 0.1% Physical.
- **Asset**: 52.5% End user device, 17.2% Account, 16.5% Server, 6.8% Network, 2.6% Other, 2.0% Cloud, 1.7% People, 0.8% Media, 0.5% Partner (Note: minor statistical variations mapped in report).

### Threat Actions in Detail
- **Misuse (44.6%)**: Top sub-categories mapped via VERIS.
- **Hacking (30.8%)**: Top sub-categories mapped via VERIS.

### False Positive Types
Incidents that raised an alert but turned out to be harmless vs. true legitimate activities.

### Mean Time to Resolve (MTTR)
- **40.6%** of True Positive incidents are confirmed and resolved within an hour of being raised.
- **72.6%** are confirmed and resolved within a day.
- On average, Priority 1 incidents are confirmed and resolved 66 hours after the initial alert was received. Incident priority can only be determined during the course of the investigation and is confirmed when the incident is closed.
- **86.6%** of incidents are confirmed and resolved within 5 days.
- Priority 1 incidents were responded to in 15 minutes on average.
- The average response time for a Priority 2 incident is 3.5 hours (abnormally high, influenced by one extreme case; adjusted P2 mean time decreases to 63 minutes from 210 minutes).
- Priority 3 incidents responded to in a little over 1.5 hours on average.
- Priority 4 incidents had an average response time of 7.5 hours.

### Small and Medium Businesses (SMB) Micro-SOC Threat Detection Data
- **Period**: Sept. 2024 - Sept. 2025
- **Scope**: 1,943 customers, ~1.5M endpoints, 1.63M resolved incidents, 835,640 (51%) confirmed as true positives.
- **Resolution**: 85% of incidents resolved within 1 hour; 4% remained unresolved. Human oversight remains essential for nuanced or multi-stage intrusions.

### Vulnerability Scanning Data (VOC Operations)
- **Period**: October 2024 to September 2025.
- **Volume**: 1,289,451 findings on 60,837 assets.
- **Findings**: Most vulnerabilities remain open for ~6 months. Increasing age of findings is a recurring theme.
- **Hidden Cost in Security Products**: 19,125 support tickets on vulnerabilities in VPNs, firewalls, etc., from 25 vendors. Each ticket generates multiple tasks (3-4 hrs/month/customer), with an average increase of tasks of 14% month-over-month, showing defensive technologies contribute to systemic cybersecurity strain.

### Cyber Extortion (Cy-X) Monitoring Data
- **Dark Net Surveillance**: Observations on double-extortion leak sites tracking victims and actors over time, regional shifts, and top 10 most active actors in 2025 (Q1 to Q3).
- **Victim Sizes**: Categorized across brands and overall volumes (1-49, 50-249, 250+).

## Hacktivism

### How did we get here? The Eras of Hacktivism[^1]
1. **Digital Utopia (Mid-1980s)**: Free access to information, privacy rights, "For the lulz", Chaos Computer Club.
2. **Anti-establishment (Mid-2000s)**: Declaring for governments or religious institutions, disrupting private corporations and corrupt governments, ethical hacking.
3. **Establishment Era (2014 and Onward)**: More cynical and reactionary, governments tolerate or even support, targeting national infrastructure, IT Army of Ukraine, NoName057(16), CyberAveng3rs.

[^1]: Derbyshire et al. (Sept, 2025) _From Protest to Power Plant: Interpreting the Role of Escalatory Hacktivism in Cyber Conflict_. [www.arxiv.org/pdf/2509.05104](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2509.05104)

### Key Trends (Nordics in the Crosshairs)
1. Surge in attack volume.
2. Increased consolidation and coordination.
3. Correlation with real-world events (Elections, #OpSweden, #OpDenmark, etc.).
4. Targets of symbolic value designed to erode societal trust rather than targets of opportunity.

## Panel Debate & Strategic Themes

The panel debate addressed five major themes where cybersecurity intersects with geopolitics, business continuity, and societal responsibility:

1. **Supply Chain Attacks**: From individual risk management to collective responsibility.
2. **SMBs in the Crosshairs**: Why attackers target smaller businesses.
3. **Cyber Extortion to Cyber Activism**: When geopolitics meets DDoS.
4. **AI (Shield, Sword, or Bomb)**: The dual-use nature of artificial intelligence in cybersecurity.
5. **Digital Sovereignty**: Is Europe alone at home? Addressing technological dependency, cloud security, and regulatory frameworks (NIS2, DORA, GDPR).

### Key Quotes & Perspectives
- **Jensen Huang (CEO Nvidia)**: Highlighted China's massive rise in AI research, patents, and STEM education, noting a complete flip in global technological leadership over the past decade.
- **Ursula von der Leyen (President of the European Commission)**: Emphasized that a European AI and independent digital infrastructure are essential for future independence, setting our own standards and ensuring Europe is not merely a consumer market for U.S. or Chinese technologies.
- **Charl van der Walt (Global Head of Security Research)**: _"In a fragmenting world, Europe's cybersecurity future depends on moving beyond strategic dependency toward resilient, independent capabilities... Resilience, not isolation, will define Europe's digital strength in the years ahead."_

### Trusted Cloud & European Control
- **Bleu**: A joint venture between Orange and Capgemini delivering Microsoft Azure and Microsoft 365 in a Trusted Cloud designed for organizations with high security and data sovereignty requirements. Operating under European jurisdiction, moving toward SecNumCloud 3.2 certification (ANSSI), and approved by the European Commission.

## Takeaways
Cybersicherheit is no longer just about IT, technology, and firewalls. It is about dependencies, connectedness, business continuity, societal resilience, and strategic leadership:
- SMB security impacts enterprise organizations.
- Supplier maturity impacts entire value chains.
- Geopolitics drives threat landscapes and attack risks.
- AI influences complexity, attack velocity, and defense capabilities.
- Technological dependence dictates our actual level of control.
