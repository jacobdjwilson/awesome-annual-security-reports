3 STATE OF PRIVACY 2026
A B S T R A C T
State of Privacy 2026 reports the results of the ISACA global State of Privacy survey conducted late in the third quarter
of 2025. This report focuses on privacy staffing, operations, and budgets; privacy awareness training; privacy breaches;
and privacy by design. State of Privacy 2026 also explores how privacy professionals are adopting artificial intelligence
(AI) and criteria that make it easier to adopt AI for privacy tasks. Many of the survey findings are consistent with last
year’s survey results, but this year’s results show that challenges related to budget cuts and staffing have worsened.
