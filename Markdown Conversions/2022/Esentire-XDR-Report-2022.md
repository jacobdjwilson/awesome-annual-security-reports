INTRODUCTION
Extended Detection and Response or XDR takes an evolving approach to threat detection and response
capabilities by bringing additional context to external threat intelligence and the internal business
environment. It ingests, enriches, and contextualizes data to provide holistic protection against
cyberattacks, unauthorized access, and misuse by breaking down traditional security silos across all data
sources. In doing so, it extends the enhanced visibility and threat containment functionality traditionally
provided by point solutions like NDR and EDR across the complete IT ecosystem.
eSentire and Cybersecurity Insiders conducted a comprehensive survey of 227 cybersecurity
professionals to reveal the latest trends and challenges in managed XDR, why and how organizations
invest in XDR services, and the security capabilities companies are prioritizing.
Key findings include:
• Contextualizing and correlating endpoint, network, cloud, identity, user behavior, and email
data are the top challenges organizations are looking to address through XDR (64%). Since XDR
also automatically blocks high fidelity threats, 59% of organizations are turning to XDR to move
beyond contextualizing and correlating data across multiple signals to automate threat detection
and initiate the remediation process.
• Over 70% of respondents agree that XDR is an important foundational platform that MDR
providers and MSSPs should offer to enable effective multi-signal MDR services that remediate
threats at speed.
• Organizations consider the lack of skilled security staff (52%) their top challenge, followed by
detection of advanced threats (48%), and speed of detection and response (46%).
• Almost half of the survey respondents take between three to five days to detect and contain
a cyber threat. An alarming majority of organizations (69%) report that their current managed
services provider responds to threats on their behalf only up to 50% of the time.

XDR BENEFITS AND PROVIDER
CONSIDERATIONS
XDR platforms should be considered a foundational component to multi-signal MDR that delivers complete
threat detection, containment, and response. There are several benefits to implementing XDR in-house or
engaging an MDR provider that leverages XDR, including:
Highly efficient Security Operations Center (SOC) support
Offering the advantage of highly efficient 24/7 Security Operations Center (SOC)
support that pushes new detection and containment knowledge when new threats
emerge. For example, XDR offers increased threat surface visibility and enables rapid
correlation and improvements in time to detect and overall detection rate. These
capabilities are accomplished by correlating signal data for high-fidelity detections.
Automation to drive higher levels of efficiency and security operations scale
Driving higher levels of efficiency by tuning detections to business context,
automatically blocking attacks, and applying machine learning models to cut noise and
reduce false positives. These capabilities enable an organization’s security operations
to scale, permitting their SOC analysts to focus on higher priority security events that
require human intuition and hand-to-hand combat.
Greater visibility and insight to accelerate threat hunting and response
Accelerating threat hunting and response with threat intel and context about
security threats. XDR providers with deep machine learning expertise can build new
AI models to automate detection and containment measures more effectively as
new threats emerge.
Superior XDR providers partner with best-of-breed technology providers that can seamlessly integrate
into their solution across multiple signals, including endpoint, log, cloud, and identity data sources. It is
also essential to know if the provider integrates their own threat intelligence from proactive threat hunting,
open-source intelligence, and commercial threat feeds to inform the XDR-driven detections. Finally,
determine if “bringing your own licenses” is available; otherwise, switching to new technology platforms
might be required.
