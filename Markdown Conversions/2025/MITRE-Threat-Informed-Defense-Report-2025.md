ABOUT THE CENTER FOR
THREAT-INFORMED DEFENSE
The Center is a non-profit research and
development consortium operated by
MITRE. The Center’s mission is to advance
the state of the art and the state of the
practice in threat-informed defense globally.
Comprised of participant organizations
from around the globe with sophisticated
security teams, the Center builds on MITRE
ATT&CK, an important foundation for threat-
informed defense used by security teams
and vendors in their enterprise security
operations. Because the Center operates for
the public good, outputs of its research and
development are available publicly and for the
benefit of all.
