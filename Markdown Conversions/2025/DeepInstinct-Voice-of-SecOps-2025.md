Eye on AI: Understanding AI terminology
Artificial Intelligence (AI) Deep Learning
Artificial Intelligence, also known as AI, is an umbrella term covering all methods and Deep learning is a family of algorithms within machine learning characterized by the use
disciplines that result in any form of intelligence exhibited by machines. Nearly all of neural networks to train and analyze data. Whereas machine learning requires users
software that’s currently being used in just about all industries employs some form to extract, compute, and vectorize a subsection of the available information to feed

cybersecurity, deep learning autonomously predicts threats to stop unknown malware
and zero-day attacks by identifying and preventing them before they can execute.
AI Agents
An AI agent (also referred to as agentic AI, or self-driving AI) is an autonomous

entity that perceives its environment through sensors and acts upon that
environment through actuators to achieve its goals. These goals can range from Machine learning is the leading subfield of AI, where systems are trained to

Adversarial AI features the system should focus on.
Adversarial AI refers to cyber threats that seek to inhibit the performance of AI
or machine learning (ML) systems by manipulating or misleading them. It’s one of
many techniques (in addition to automated hacking, AI-powered phishing, evasion
techniques, deepfakes, etc.) that bad actors use to inflict harm.
Dark AI
Dark AI is the programming of AI to carry out malicious activities such
as cyberattacks.
Generative AI (GenAI)
GenAI refers to algorithms or deep learning models that can be used to create
new content, including text, images, and video. ChatGPT, Claude, and DALL-E are
examples of GenAI.
